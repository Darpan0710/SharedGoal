Questions

Good to know.

What is SharedGoal?

SharedGoal is a collaborative platform to collect money and make group decisions around meaningful moments — from friends chipping in for a birthday gift or vacation to communities supporting verified causes.

Do I need an account to start?

No. You can define your occasion, title, description, target amount and deadline first. Google login is requested seamlessly before finalizing and inviting members.

Who can see my goal?

It depends on the visibility you choose. Private goals are strictly limited to the creator and invited members. Public goals are open for anyone on SharedGoal to discover and support.

What is a private goal?

A private goal is designed for personal moments like birthdays, weddings, trips, and farewells. Only invited contributors can view the goal, see member amounts, and vote on gift options.

What is a public goal?

Public goals appear on our "Help Someone" directory and are discoverable by the broader community. They are typically used for education funds, medical emergencies, and community assistance.

How do contributions work?

Members can contribute using UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), digital payment methods, or recorded cash handoffs that are verified by the goal creator or group admins.

What happens after completion?

Once the target is met or deadline is reached, the creator records the final spending outcome. Any remaining funds can be accounted for or reconciled with the group transparently.

How are Help Someone requests verified?

Every public help request goes through an audit process where proof documents (invoices, hospital estimates, student IDs) are reviewed by admins before the request is published.
